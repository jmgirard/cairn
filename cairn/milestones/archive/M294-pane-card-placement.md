# M294: A candidate row's ⓘ card opens where the cairn pane has room for it

**Status:** done (2026-10-20, PR #307 https://github.com/jmgirard/cairn/pull/307)

**Goal:** On the desktop, a candidate row's ⓘ card opens above its row when it fits there, else below it, and is cut to the larger side only when it fits on neither.

**Outcome:** `hooks/status/pane.ts` gains `cardRows` (rows a card's text takes: a greedy wrap at spaces at `bodyColumns` less `CARD_FRAME_COLUMNS` (4), in code points, plus 2 border rows), `cardPlace` (up when it fits above, else down, else the larger side cut, above on a tie, none under `CARD_MIN_ROWS` (3)), `cardPlaces` (from the Pane render props `bodyColumns`, `scroll.offset`, `scroll.bodyRows`, `placement`; inline room is the fewer of `bodyRows` and the tree's rows), and `cardCut` (the longest run of whole words that fits with ` …`). Each candidate line carries `rowsAbove` (its index plus the summary card's 2 border rows). `register.tsx`'s `cardBox` draws an up card in its line at `bottom: 1` and a down card after every line in the `cairn-pane` Box at `top: rowsAbove + 1`, revealed only by the ⓘ group; a cut card holds `cardCut`'s text and sets no height. Mod tests went from 2336 to 2389. README, DESIGN, the unreleased ⓘ-card CHANGELOG entry, and the comments state the rule.

**Decisions:** none promoted. Question set: a card too tall for either side is cut to the larger side; a live look in circumplex. Plan gate: render props over tracking `ui.scroll` events; an overlay at the pane's end over an in-line card at `top: 1`. Substantive amendment at the live look, approved by the operator: AC5 cuts the text by whole words with no fixed height, since a fixed height left empty rows.
