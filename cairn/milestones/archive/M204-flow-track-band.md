# M204: A flow track band: plan, implement, review

**Status:** done (2026-10-04, PR #211 https://github.com/jmgirard/cairn/pull/211)

**Goal:** On the desktop surface, draw the band's progress as one rounded track whose three equal segments run plan, implement, and review, with a pixel-texture fill, a pill label at the fill's edge, tick marks, and a percent.

**Outcome:** `band.ts` gains the flow model: `flowOf`, `idleFlow`, `planFlow`, and `percentOf`, with whole-number fills and `floor(100 × sum / 3)`. Plan is full on a milestone row, implement fills by tasks, and review fills by criteria. A new `track.ts` builds the SVG in design A: a translucent gray ground and 2-pixel specks to the active phase's fill edge, thickening and taking the phase color toward it. It adds edge marks at the thirds, ticks for items k with checked < k < n when they sit 6 pixels apart or more, and a pill with a bold label and dim count at the edge. It uses one palette and no `prefers-color-scheme` rule. `register.tsx` draws the `Svg` on the desktop only, 360 px wide, in a centered row. The fit counts it as `TRACK_COLUMNS` 52, and below that the row takes the terminal's text forms. The idle row and a `/milestone-plan` skill row with no active milestone draw the track. `/milestone-plan`'s label is `rgb(110,140,190)` on both surfaces. The old suites run on the terminal, and new walks cover the desktop. Mod tests went from 600 to 944. README, CHANGELOG, and DESIGN describe the track.

**Decisions:** The plan gate chose equal thirds over item weighting and kept the terminal text row. A cell-grid track became the row "Terminal flow track". It kept cairn's colors with a new plan blue. At the live look the operator rejected the first look, solid dithered segments, as a lazy adaptation of the reference screenshot. From three browser-pane prototypes the operator picked A, over finer specks and over color in the pill only. Scope and AC2 were amended. AC2's wording took two re-audits and went to the operator, who accepted it.
