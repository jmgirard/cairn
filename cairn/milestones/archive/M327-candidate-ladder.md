# M327: Let triage move a row to any rung

**Status:** done (2026-10-30, PR #340 https://github.com/jmgirard/cairn/pull/340)

**Goal:** Triage and every skill that writes or updates a candidate row can propose any place on one ladder, with a reason, and the user decides.

**Outcome:** The rulebook's "Candidate priority token" paragraph states the ladder (high, normal, low, parked, Known issue, dropped). A move can cross any number of rungs either way. Only parked needs a condition, and a skill can propose a Known issues entry or no row at its own gate. `/cairn-triage` step 2 replaces M326's No condition class with a Rung class: any rung for any row that no started token or live milestone holds. A move to parked drafts a condition that has not held, and a move off parked needs none held. Readiness promotes on a judged reason, Misfiling routes either way, *not worth keeping* is a judged reason for any item, §7 options take a rung change, and a fired trigger whose work is undone goes to Readiness. Seven gate sentences are gone. A kept mark, ` — kept YYYY-MM-DD`, is written at Accept when the user keeps or declines a proposed move. It filters every class and §7, it is placed where a merge lineage clause goes, and it lifts only on a record dated after it. It is wired through the chip, Accept, the sixth carve-out, skip, size, stamp, and status. A route up writes the named rung's token. `/milestone-review` places follow-ups on a rung (default parked, "promote when a user reports it") and can propose a Known issues entry. Its `known issue:` Review line is accepted by the merge or handoff approval and written at step 9. `/copilot-review`, records-hygiene §7, `/milestone`'s stale-row chip, README, CHANGELOG, re-pinned guards, and the rulebook-mass seed (855 / 84,618) follow.

**Decisions:** D-228 supersedes D-227's forced-condition, *not worth keeping*, and `[high]` clauses. It narrows D-184's writer rule and D-149's Known issues rejection, and annotates D-027, D-134, and D-205. The user chose at the question set that triage and every row writer can propose any outcome, any number of rungs. AC6 was amended to check what each skill version allows or requires (re-audited twice).

**Review:** Criteria audit at plan (two rounds, 9 findings taken). Claim audit 58 claims, 2 corrected. Two dry runs at implement and one at review, under the plan-commit and head skills. Two review passes of four lenses, with one floor return for the route-up trigger, the Known issues record, and guest acceptance. 76 findings in all: 47 fixed, 29 rejected (planned ladder behavior, scope, style, or false), no follow-ups. Copilot: requests not recorded, no review. Hygiene: no row graduated or unparked; M324 row pruned; `used M327` on five lessons; one lesson captured (dry-run criteria for judgment designs).
