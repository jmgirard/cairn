# M329: Flag maintainer decisions at the plan

**Status:** done (2026-11-02, PR #343 https://github.com/jmgirard/cairn/pull/343)

**Goal:** In guest mode, a plan poses a needed maintainer decision before it goes on, and review shows a reported planned removal and a render.

**Outcome:** Guest `/milestone-plan` step 2 asks whether work needs a maintainer's decision: a feature, a change of behavior, a removal or hiding of output, or a design choice. It reads issues the request or a row names or the sweep matches, plus the operator's PRs in any state (`gh pr list --author @me --state all --limit 100`; a full list counts as a failed read), and the operator's own text is never agreement. A maintainer's no outranks an agreement that does not answer it, and a close as not planned counts when `closed_by` is neither a bot nor the author. Step 3 poses one question before any file: draft a question, a maintainer already agreed, or do not build. Wait takes the draft's place only where the user's own question has no answer since. A draft goes to `cairn-question-<owner>-<repo>-<slug>.md` in the system temp directory and the close block, and the user posts it. Step 4 writes `maintainer decision:` (agreement, `named by the user`, `none`, or `declined`). `/milestone-review` step 5 gains the guest-only `planned removal` ground, with what users lose and a count or a condition, and step 7 shows it. Step 7's guest render paragraph runs when a user-facing surface's snapshot tests fail or skip at both the head and the merge base: a before and after render from each tree's own code and a `render:` line. Step 8 limits what a PR says about a snapshot and says when no maintainer agreed. Rule (4) in tracking-rules, the session-start hook text, README, and CHANGELOG follow, and the rulebook seed is 892 lines / 88,339 bytes.
