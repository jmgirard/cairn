# M276: A feature cut short at implement or review still ships as one change

**Status:** done (2026-10-18, PR #288 https://github.com/jmgirard/cairn/pull/288)

**Goal:** When an amendment at implement or a descope at review moves part of a user-facing milestone to later work, the cut milestone and the milestone that finishes it form a feature series, so the feature keeps one changelog entry and the release walk sees it as unfinished.

**Outcome:** tracking-rules gains an "A cut series" paragraph after "Feature series": a scope cut (a substantive amendment moving work a user-facing milestone's Goal or Scope `In:` promised, at implement or carrying out a review descope) makes the cut milestone and the milestone later planned for that work a series; rules (a) and (b) do not bind the cut milestone; its changelog entry is the one entry under (c); only the later milestone carries the `feature series:` line. `/milestone-implement` step 6: the cut writes the moved work as a candidate row with ` — split from M<NNN>` after its first `: ` (the same row when its home is a split milestone); no marker on a narrowed criterion's follow-up row or an internal-tier remainder; a review descope's remainder takes it. `/milestone-plan` step 4: a milestone planned from a marked row gets `feature series: M<NNN> -> M<new>` and edits the cut milestone's entry; the "never a candidate row" clause excepts a cut series. `/cairn-release` step 1 names a marked row whose cut milestone is `done` as an unplanned part, leaving out one whose started token names a live milestone.
