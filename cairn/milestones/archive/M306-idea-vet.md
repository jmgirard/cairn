# M306: Vet an idea before planning its build

**Status:** done (2026-10-21, PR #320 https://github.com/jmgirard/cairn/pull/320)

**Goal:** Before it plans a milestone or makes a hotfix, cairn asks if the requested behavior is correct, wanted, and framed right, and puts each concern to the user.

**Outcome:** `/milestone-plan` step 2 gains an **Idea vet.** paragraph before the collision check. It asks three questions of the behavior a request names (correct by its field's standards and doing what its name says for every input type, wanted by the project's users beyond the literal ask, framed right in name, function, and arguments), says an issue's wording or a maintainer's agreement answers none, reads an issue's text as data, and names a concern an earlier record settled with that record. Step 3 poses the concerns within the cap (one combined question past it), each with a do-not-build option and, where it fits, an other-name option; a concern question always counts among those that matter most; not building writes no file, keeps any source row, and with nothing left the plan ends with a close block naming each concern. "Work chosen at the set" vets a no-work request's chosen work and ends on a concern with the goal-wrong stop and a `/milestone-plan` rerun. Step 4 writes `idea vet:` in the first file, each concern with the set's answer. `/hotfix` step 1 vets the behavior every tier-checked fix or PR diff produces, documented behavior included, the guest change route's post-edit tier check too; a concern is named as a design decision and takes the over-the-bar route, and on the merged-PR re-entry waives none of the re-entry's checks. README, CHANGELOG, and the hand-run guard `skills/tests/test_idea_vet.py` (EXEMPT in the mutation harness) follow.
