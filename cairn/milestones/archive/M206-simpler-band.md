# M206: A simpler band: id, title, and the track

**Status:** done (2026-10-04, PR #213 https://github.com/jmgirard/cairn/pull/213)

**Goal:** Make the band's row the milestone id and title on the left and the flow track on the right, on every surface and at every width.

**Outcome:** A band row in `band.ts` is now `{ id, title, tail, track? }`. The left group is the bold id and the title. The phase and skill labels, the `→` with the chapter or next open item, the skill row, the idle row's `next`, the ten-cell bar and its counts forms, `planFlow`, and `SKILL_LABELS` are gone, and `CAIRN_SKILLS` lists the skills. The track takes the columns the row leaves after the title's 10, at most 52 and at least 12. `Flow.short` (the counts, `none`, or `Planned`) shows when the whole pill would take more than a third of the track. `trackSvg` takes a width, min(360, columns × 7). The terminal draws `brailleSpans`: braille cells on `userMessageBackground`, specks in `inactive` or the phase color, a white bold pill on the phase color, and a `subtle` mark at each third. Every other surface draws the image. The chapter-tool hook went, and `CairnStep` lost `chapter` (shape `step-2`). With no active row the idle row shows under any skill. The close state keeps `milestone-review` only where it moves the row (shape `dismissed-4`). An Edit, Write, or MultiEdit under `cairn/` re-reads the files. `band.test.tsx` was rewritten, and mod tests went from 1136 to 853, with sweeps from 40 to 200 columns. README, DESIGN, and CHANGELOG describe the row.

**Decisions:** The question set chose a terminal track over count text, a shorter image track for narrow windows, and no skill row. The operator rejected two terminal looks and picked braille specks from a second round of browser prototypes, and picked narrow variant A. AC3 and AC4 each took two audit passes with findings. The operator chose narrower wording for both, so the look is judged at the live look. The live look accepted all three views.

**Review:** Three lenses gave 24 findings. 12 were fixed: the image off the terminal, a skill's start or end no longer showing a hidden band, the shape tag, the mid-turn refresh lost with the chapter hook, the exact pill in the terminal sweep, a vacuous pane test, and four comment or doc slips. 8 went to the new row "Band follow-ons (M206 review)" or existing rows: narrow widths under 40, the column estimate, and themes. 4 were rejected. A claim audit read 140 claims and corrected 5. The "Terminal flow track" row was absorbed and pruned. One lesson was extended.
