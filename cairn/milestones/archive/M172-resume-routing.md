# M172: A merged or stopped review milestone resumes at the right step

**Status:** done (2026-09-02, PR #175 https://github.com/jmgirard/cairn/pull/175)

**Goal:** A review or hotfix session that stopped at the CI wait, or whose PR was merged outside the session, re-enters at the step the record shows is next, so post-merge hygiene is never skipped by accident.

**Outcome:** `/milestone-review` Session start "Resume routing (M172)": when the header carries a PR URL, `gh pr view <N> --json state,mergedAt` is read before step 1 and the session routes (a) merged, every box ticked against evidence, approval line recorded → resume work-log line, steps 9–10; (b) merged otherwise → steps 3–7 post-hoc against the merged head (reviewers on `gh pr diff <N>`, fix-now via `/hotfix`, docs-only checkpoint, an accept-not-merge chip), then steps 9–10; (c) open, ticked, approved → step 1 re-run and pushed, chip re-posed, step 8 from the marker; (d) anything else → step 1 with step 2 skipping `gh pr create` for an open PR; `gh` unavailable → step 1, recap naming which. Step 7 appends `step-7 approval: PR #<N> approved for merge`, committed and pushed before the marker. tracking-rules "On timeout" ends in a close block naming the invoking skill's command; "Resume is stateless" re-derives merge state via `gh pr view <N> --json state`; `/milestone-review` step 8, `/hotfix` step 6, `/cairn-release` step 3 restate it. `/milestone` §2 reports a `review` milestone with a merged PR as hygiene owed; `/hotfix` step 1 runs step 7 only for a merged PR whose head is not `m<nnn>-*`, with its own issue-close chip. `skills/tests/test_resume_routing.py` (hand-run, D-109) pins the trigger, the four routes whole with whitespace collapsed, the approval line, the audit bullet and its adjacency, and the hotfix re-entry; nine mutation entries.
