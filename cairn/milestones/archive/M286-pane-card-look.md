# M286: The cairn pane opens with a repo summary card, wraps goals at its width, and takes a new look

**Status:** done (2026-10-18, PR #298 https://github.com/jmgirard/cairn/pull/298)

**Goal:** The cairn pane opens with a summary card of the repo's pull requests, issues, candidate rows, and milestones, wraps each milestone goal at the pane's width, and draws its headings, Next line, and pull-request rows in one look that the operator picks.

**Outcome:** A round-bordered card leads the pane (`cardLines` in `pane.ts`): open PRs and issues from one `gh api graphql` `totalCount` query on a github.com base remote (`repo.ts` `repoArgv`/`repoCounts`, `?` until a read gives numbers, last good numbers kept per folder), candidate rows per level through `drawnCandidates`, the filter the Candidates list shares, planned and blocked counts, and outside guest mode a release line from `git describe`, `git log %ct`, and `git diff --no-renames` over the archive (`doneSince`). `readRepo` runs from `readPrs` beside the hotfix list, writes last, and stores the `repo` atom (shape `repo-2`). ⌂ and ↻ moved to the card. `goalBlocks` joins a goal paragraph's lines and keeps list items apart. Mod tests went from 2184 to 2244. README, DESIGN, CHANGELOG, and `types/index.d.ts` describe it.
