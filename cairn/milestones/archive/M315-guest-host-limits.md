# M315: Keep guest sessions within the host's limits

**Status:** done (2026-10-22, PR #329 https://github.com/jmgirard/cairn/pull/329)

**Goal:** `/milestone-review` and `/hotfix` check two caps on the operator's open PRs on a repo, one for bug fixes and one for features, before they open a new PR, in owner and guest mode. In a repo the operator does not own, sessions also follow set rules for review threads, bot comments, other people's issues, and agreeing on a feature's design with a maintainer first.

**Outcome:** The tracking-rules git model gains an open-PR caps bullet: at most 3 of your open PRs from `hotfix-` branches and 1 other per repo, changed by the `# Bug-fix PR cap: N` and `# Feature PR cap: N` header lines of `cairn/PROFILE.md` (`[1-9][0-9]?`, read as the collaboration-mode line). A PR counts by head branch; a stacked milestone's base PR and the review's own PR are not counted; a 100-PR list counts as both caps reached. `/hotfix` step 1 reads the bug-fix cap for an authored fix before its branch; `/milestone-review` reads the feature cap at step 2's Copilot arm, before step 7's chip (owner), and before step 8's push (guest). At a cap or on a failed read they push nothing and stop; a `/hotfix` stop adds a candidate row. `hooks/session_context.py` adds an `## Open-PR caps` part in both modes (`cairn_common.open_pr_caps`, naming an untaken value as written) and, in guest mode, `HOST_RULES`: no resolve of a person's thread, no bot reply beyond the one Copilot comment, issue closes only on clear grounds with a one-line comment and at most 3 per UTC day (counted from `issues/events`), and a feature planned only after a maintainer agreed with its design. README, CHANGELOG, DESIGN's header-line list, and `/milestone`'s rulebook-mass baseline (820 lines / 80,287 bytes) follow.
