## Goal

`/milestone-review` and `/hotfix` check two caps on the operator's open PRs on a repo, one for bug fixes and one for features, before they open a new PR, in owner and guest mode. In a repo the operator does not own, sessions also follow set rules for review threads, bot comments, other people's issues, and agreeing on a feature's design with a maintainer first.

## Scope

**In:** two open-PR caps, read from the `# Bug-fix PR cap: N` (default 3) and `# Feature PR cap: N` (default 1) header lines of `cairn/PROFILE.md`, checked in owner and guest mode by `/milestone-review` and `/hotfix` before a new PR opens; a PR counts by its head branch. The caps in the session-start text in both modes, and four guest rules in the guest session-start text (`hooks/session_context.py`, with a cap reader in `hooks/cairn_common.py`) and in tracking-rules "Collaboration mode": no resolve of a thread a person started, no reply to a bot, limits on closing another person's issue, and a feature or a change of behavior planned only after a maintainer agrees with its design. README, CHANGELOG, DESIGN's header-line list, and a D-entry.

**Out:** the one-comment Copilot reply → M314. When the Copilot round runs: guest repos keep the opt-in line (the user's answer). A hook that blocks `gh pr create` or `gh issue close` by count: the rules are instructions to the session, and only the two skills check the caps. The caps at `/milestone-implement`'s branch cut: the handoff is where a PR opens. `/hotfix`'s follow-up PR after its merged-PR re-entry, its re-land of a contributor's work, and companion PRs read no cap: each carries work from a PR that already exists. A check in `/milestone-plan` that an issue shows a maintainer's agreement: the issue-first rule is an instruction that applies before a plan starts (the user's answer). A guest repo where the operator merges their own PRs (easystats/datawizard) → candidate row.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] AC1: In owner and guest mode, `/milestone-review` and `/hotfix` read the operator's open PRs on `<base-repo>` with `gh pr list --repo <base-repo> --author @me --state open --limit 100 --json number,url,headRefName` at these sites: in `/milestone-review`, step 2's Copilot arm, before step 7's merge chip is posed in owner mode (step 8 opens the PR on approval), and before step 8's push in guest mode after its push-access and stack-base reads, each only when that step will open a PR: the header names no open PR and the review is not on resume route (b); in `/hotfix`, step 1 for an authored fix from a bug report, before step 2 cuts its branch (not the merged-PR re-entry's follow-up). A listed PR counts by its head branch, not its content: a `headRefName` that starts with `hotfix-` is a bug fix and any other a feature, and a stacked milestone's `<base-pr>` is not counted. `/hotfix` compares the bug-fix count with the bug-fix cap, and `/milestone-review` the feature count with the feature cap. A count at its cap or over it makes the skill push nothing, open no PR, leave any milestone status as it is, and stop with a close block that names the cap and each open PR of that kind; a list of 100 PRs counts as reaching both caps, and its close block says the list reached 100 in place of the PRs. A failed read stops the same way, naming the read and its error in place of the open PRs. `/milestone-review`'s close block fences `/milestone-review M<NNN>`, labeled as rerunning the review. A push to an already-open PR reads no cap. The bug-fix cap is N from a `# Bug-fix PR cap: N` line, and the feature cap N from a `# Feature PR cap: N` line, in the header of `cairn/PROFILE.md`, each read as the `# Collaboration mode:` line is read, when N matches `[1-9][0-9]?`, and 3 and 1 otherwise; the close block names each cap line in the header whose value it did not take.
- [ ] AC2: In owner and guest mode, the session-start text that `hooks/session_context.py` injects, in a part ahead of the ROADMAP part, states at most B of your open PRs from `hotfix-` branches and at most F of your other open PRs on the repo, counting every open PR you authored there, and a PR stacked on another of yours as one with it, B and F being AC1's caps; it names each cap line in the header whose value it did not take. In guest mode it also states four rules. (1) Never resolve a review thread that a person started. (2) Reply to no bot, except the one comment per Copilot review with a note that the Copilot round or `/copilot-review` posts. (3) Close an issue someone else opened only when a merged change clearly fixes it or its fix belongs in another repo, with a one-line comment naming that change or repo, which holds over host style matching, and at most 3 such closes on that repo per UTC day, counting your closes there that day; when unsure, ask the user in chat and post nothing. A close the user picks at a cairn chip is the user's act and is outside rule (3). (4) Before you plan a feature or a change of behavior, find where a maintainer (an author whose association is OWNER, MEMBER, or COLLABORATOR) agreed with its design in an issue on the host repo, or asked for it in a review of your PR; with neither, tell the user in chat and plan nothing until a maintainer agrees. A fix that restores documented behavior is outside rule (4). For these inputs the injected text reads as follows (cases in `hooks/tests`): "at most 5 of your open PRs from `hotfix-` branches" and "at most 2 of your other open PRs" for `# Bug-fix PR cap: 5` and `# Feature PR cap: 2` lines, in guest mode and in owner mode; "at most 99 …" for `# Bug-fix PR cap: 99`; "at most 3 …" and "at most 2 …" for a `# Feature PR cap: 2` line alone; "at most 3 …" and "at most 1 …" for no lines and for the lines placed below the first `## `, with no line named; the default for `many`, `0`, and `100` on each line in turn, each value named; both values named when both lines are bad; a bad line named in owner mode; each guest rule's phrase in guest mode; and no guest rule in owner mode.
- [ ] AC3: tracking-rules states, as a rule of owner and guest mode, the two caps, their header lines and defaults, the head-branch count with its stack-base exclusion, the 100-PR rule, and where each skill reads them, and its "Collaboration mode" section states each of AC2's four guest rules. Its file-map row for `cairn/PROFILE.md` names the `# Bug-fix PR cap:` and `# Feature PR cap:` header lines and `/milestone-review`, `/hotfix`, and `hooks/session_context.py` as their readers, and still says that no validator reads them. `# Open PR cap` appears in no file under `skills/` or `hooks/`, nor in README.md, CHANGELOG.md, or cairn/DESIGN.md.
- [ ] AC4: README and `CHANGELOG.md`'s Unreleased section describe, in place of the one-cap `# Open PR cap` text, the two caps in owner and guest mode, their header lines and defaults, that they count every open PR you authored on the repo and that one counts as a bug fix only when its branch starts with `hotfix-`, that `/milestone-review` and `/hotfix` stop at a cap before opening a PR, and the four guest rules as instructions each guest session is given.
